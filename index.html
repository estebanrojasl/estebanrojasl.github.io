Anytime Sim Golf · Lead Product Engineer

May 2024 to present

Canada · Client project

Golf simulator facilities with no staff on site. The software books the bay, takes the payment, and runs the lights and the simulators. It replaced a mix of Shopify and Zapier.

The scary part was the hardware. I was in Medellín and the simulators were in Canada, so I couldn't walk over and try things. I dealt with that first, with tests, retries and fallbacks around every hardware call. Once that was solid it was a normal software project, and the biggest risk was gone before most features existed.

I owned it from the estimate and contract to production. We started as two developers and I finished it alone, on a plain TypeScript monolith so one person could move fast. I also pitched features. One was a one-click extension: 10 minutes before your session ends, if the next slot is free, you get a discount to stay. At peak hours the next slot is almost always booked, so the offer can't fire, and people were asking to extend anyway. That told me the real lever was off-peak pricing.

It's live, and the client now licenses it to other facility owners. I wrote the technical brief for the multi-tenant version.
